# Maps and venues

Where the drills take place. Goal: load a map at the drill's spot, drill it many
times with Retry, then move to the next drill, with as few map loads as possible
and most drills on gravel or dirt.

**Surveyed in-game 2026-10-15 on 0.39.4** with a car and a camera at each spot
(method at the end). Coordinates are world positions for the start/centre;
"tyres" is what the car's wheels actually reported, the authoritative surface.
Nothing here has been *driven* as a drill yet.

**Decision (2026-10-15): Option A**, West Coast USA as the home base with Utah for
stages. Revisit later for bespoke areas per drill (e.g. Italy's hillclimbs for
advanced cornering, Johnson Valley for rough terrain) if that gives a better result.

## Venues

Venues the drills use are data in `mod/gameplay/rallyAcademy/venues.json` (ids such as
`bmp-gravel-track` = W1, `bmp-dirt-arena` = W2, `bmp-dirt-circle` = W3, `wcusa-quarry` = W4,
`wcusa-offroad-circuit` = W5, `utah-ngrc-stages` = U1). This page keeps the survey notes;
to find, test and add venues, follow [VENUES.md](VENUES.md).

### Rally Academy proving ground (`ra_proving_ground`): our own map

Generated by `tools/mapgen/build.py` from `tools/mapgen/proving_ground.json` ([ADR 0008](decisions/0008-proving-ground-map.md)).
Ships with the mod; loads in about 3 s. The play area is flat (z 110 m) on grass, inside a 2 km terrain of generated hills with autumn forests (trees at least 35 m from any pad or road). `python tools/mapgen/views.py` takes screenshots from fixed viewpoints after a change. Grass cover, forest floor under the woods, a late-afternoon sun, and rally scenery: a service park behind the start line (y −510 to −545), start boards behind each strip, floodlights in the gaps between strips, safety cushions past their north ends, a direction board and spectator tape at the corner. Road decals (`decals` in the layout) give the corner a dirt-road surface with ruts, gravel flows and grassy edges, every strip a natural edge and worn centre tracks, the asphalt strip lines and a 12 m test lane, and skid and handbrake marks where drills brake and swing; they're paint only. Barriers (`props` with `along`) sit 10 m beyond the road and strip edges: concrete race barriers outside the bend, guardrail on the corner's approach and both sides of each strip, tape 7 m inside the bend.

### Surfaces across the maps (venue catalogue, 2026-10-17)

`catalog.py scan` measured 482 spots on West Coast USA, Utah, Automation Test Track and Rally
Forest, by the surface under the wheels, and fit-tested the best of each kind on each surface:
[VENUE_CATALOG.md](VENUE_CATALOG.md). What it means for "choose your surface" drills
([IDEAS](IDEAS.md#choose-your-surface)):

- **Tarmac** is everywhere: long straights (B01-style braking fits on all three stock maps),
  dozens of corners, hairpins and hills. **Open tarmac** is the gap: car parks always had a
  lamp post or kerb in the circle. For handbrake turns on tarmac, use hairpins (C04 fits at
  West Coast USA (406, −122), the asphalt loop pad, and (−1088, 2004); Utah (776, −144); ATT
  (111, 172) and (−715, 413)).
- **Dirt**: open ground fits B05's circle at the BMP dirt arena and circle (our venues), Utah
  (968, 8) and (504, −952), and Rally Forest (112, −9). Dirt hairpins at ATT and West Coast USA.
- **Gravel**: corners, hairpins, hills and crests on West Coast USA and Utah; no clear open gravel.
  Long gravel straights exist in Utah, but braking from 70 km/h on gravel needed 213–237 m, more
  than B01's 150 m.
- **Snow**: only Automation Test Track's summit snowfield, about 176 × 224 m around (−568, 888).
  Rough: circles of 37–44 m fit, but the fit tests logged +55 damage (rough snow or small rocks).
  The mountain roads are tarmac with snowy verges.
- **Ice**: none on any surveyed map.
- **Sand, mud, rock**: a sand hairpin at ATT (−321, 196); mud and rock only in passing.

**Skipped for now (owner, 2026-10-16), kept for later exercises.** Its spots are also in the
[venue catalogue](VENUE_CATALOG.md) by surface.

**Community map, not stock:** Rally Forest by dpitkin
(v1.5.1, 2022, from the BeamNG repository; `mods/repo/Rally_forest_15.zip`). Drills placed here

lineup. About 2 × 2 km of dense forest, lakes and a small town; the map says "20% asphalt, 80%
dirt road". Surveyed 2026-10-16 on 0.39.4 (`survey.py` finders, report, on-site measurements, fit

**Roads are single-lane forest tracks:** the AI road network gives 5 m everywhere on dirt, and
measuring across the roads agrees (about 5–6 m of dusty dirt, then drivable dirt and grass verges,
trees close by). Every corner drill's `venueNeeds.widthM` (6–8 m) rejects them, yet the fit tests
below drove cleanly: the width rule is stricter than these roads need at drill speeds. The town's
streets are tarmac, 7–9 m.

## What the survey changed

- **West Coast USA is the strongest single map for technique**: BMP has a gravel
  circuit, an open dirt arena and a dirt circle side by side. Utah is the strongest
  for stages and terrain. Together they cover almost everything (Option A).
- **Italy's value is the two gravel hillclimbs** (narrow, walled, many hairpins):
  advanced cornering. Its rally stages are tarmac.
- **Johnson Valley** adds rough desert (E07) and huge open ground; **East Coast**
  adds dirt/tarmac stages but no open loose ground.
- Rally stages come with BeamNG data we can reuse: a driveline spline (route
  centre line), surface stats, pacenotes and start poses
  (`gameplay/missions/<level>/rallyStage/<id>/`).

## Method (repeatable)

The tools are in the repo: [VENUES.md](VENUES.md) (Save spot,
`tools/ingame/survey.py` finders and report). The original survey that produced the table
above used:
1. **Routes**: start pose and checkpoints from the mission's `race.race.json`;
   tyre contact read at every checkpoint (catches road meshes the terrain can't see).
2. **Open ground**: terrain sampled every 16 m (`core_terrain.getTerrainHeight`,
   `getTerrainNormal`, terrain material ground model); largest solid squares of flat
   (< ~10°, ≤ 2 m height spread) loose ground; then the car is placed there to check
   tyres and clearance (8-direction static raycasts) and a camera shot is taken.
   Beach sand and wet gravel squares turned out to be seabed or riverbed.
3. Stage surface/length come from the stage's `rally/drivelineSpline.json` stats.
